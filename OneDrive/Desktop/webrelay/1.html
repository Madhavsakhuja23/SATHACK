Cooking Recipe

1. Paneer-Tikka

Ingredients

  1. 500gm Paneer
  2. Butter
  3. Onion
  4. Capsicum
  5. Mayonaise
  6. Cream
  7. Barbeque
  8. Salt
  9. Black Pepper
  10. Maida
  11. corn flour

Steps To Make

  • Take paneer and wash it with water and cut into the small cubes.

  • Take the onion and peel it and wash it with a luke warm water and cut it into the small peices according to the size of panner

  • Similarly wash the capsicum and cut it into the peices.

  • Take a bowl Wash it properly. Put the panner in it. Add maida and corn flour to it .Add few water to it accordingly and add some salt and black pepper to it according to taste.

  • After Adding salt and black pepper. Stir it until the all panner cubes is coated with the paste of corn flour and maida.

  • Now ready the barbeque. Add panner cubes,onion and capsicum to the stick of the barbeque and add a layer of Butter coating to the ready stick.

  • Now Start baking Your Ready stick.Till it become reddish Brown. Now When the stick is ready Take the stick and cut the peices of panner,onion and capsicum accordingly And put them in a bowl And add cream,salt,Blck pepper and stir it.

  • Now take the Ready panner tikka in the palte. and garnish it wirth
